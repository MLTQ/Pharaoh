# BibleTab.tsx

## Purpose
The show's core: title, format, logline, premise, tone, themes, world, continuity (episodic resets / serialized binds), episode length, and the **formula**: ordered beats with descriptions, reorderable, with a preset per format (`FORMULA_PRESETS` in `lib/archetypes.ts`). An empty formula tells the writer to use the format's classic shape.
