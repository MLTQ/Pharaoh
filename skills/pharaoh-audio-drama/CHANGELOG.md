# Changelog

## 2.0.0 — Portable distribution

- Bundle the skill with Pharaoh, with prerequisites and agent-installation guidance.
- Replace installation-specific paths, cast IDs and personal production history
  with configured variables, generic guidance and fictional examples.
- Distinguish adaptation defaults, optional audition recipes and version-dependent
  CLI quirks from universally applicable behavior.
- Require explicit source delivery at the start of speech instructions; keep
  acoustic performance review separate from transcription checks.
- Preserve practical safeguards for references, row binding, thought effects,
  supporting-effect overlap, relayout and in-context review.
- Add a miniature performance pilot, a configurable read-only FFmpeg probe, and
  a standard-library bundle validator.
- Explain backend capability/licence limits and avoid universal deployment claims.

## Earlier internal edition

Production guidance developed through iterative audio-drama adaptation, directing,
voice audition, mixing and delivery. Superseded for public distribution by 2.0.0.
